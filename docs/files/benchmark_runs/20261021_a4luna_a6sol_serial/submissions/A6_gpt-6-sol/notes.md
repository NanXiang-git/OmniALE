# Transcription notes

The video and its natural audio were analyzed together. Short-time spectral pitch rises supplied onset candidates; the keyboard view constrained their registers, and low-note harmonics were filtered against independent pitch rises. This is a transcription of the supplied performance, including repeated attacks, rather than a reconstruction from an edition.

Notation uses 4/4 at quarter note = 120. Onsets were placed on a sixteenth-note grid, with video time 0 aligned to score time 0. The first counted attack occurs after opening rests; attacks outside [1.230, 75.480) are not included. Written durations organize the voices for reading and are not estimates of pedal release.

Dense chords and quiet upper notes remain the least certain, especially where a soft note coincides with a strong low-note overtone. Hand assignment uses a pragmatic split near middle C.
